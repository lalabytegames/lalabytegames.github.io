Neon Rush - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Neon Rush ("the game") is made by LalaByte Games, an independent developer (Adrian Lala, Albania). This policy explains what information the game handles. Questions: lalabytegames@gmail.com.

Information the game itself stores

Your scores, coins, unlocked cars, missions and settings are saved on your device. The developer does not collect them, has no servers, and does not see them. Using "Reset progress" in Settings deletes them.

So that your progress can move to a new phone, this save file is included in Android's own backup: if backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android stores it encrypted in your Google Account and restores it when you install the game on a new or reset phone, or copies it during a direct phone-to-phone transfer. This is handled by Google and Android under Google's privacy policy; the developer cannot access it. You can turn backup off in your device settings or delete backups in your Google Account.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your device settings),
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • App interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic / performance data.

Data is encrypted in transit. How Google uses this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Google Play Games

If you sign in with Google Play Games, your Play Games profile (gamertag and avatar), your scores and the achievements you unlock are sent to Google to show leaderboards and achievements. Google also collects analytics and diagnostics for the service. You can choose who sees your profile and delete your Play Games data in your Play Games profile or Google Account.

Sharing your score

The share button creates a picture of your score on your device and opens Android's share menu. Nothing is sent unless you choose an app and send it yourself.

Your choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, Switzerland or a region with similar rules, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also opt out of personalised ads for all apps in your device's Google / Ads settings.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
